## Word #1 - Achieve

**Pronunciation:** /əˈtʃiːv/

**Part of Speech:** Verb

**Meaning (Simple):**
To successfully reach a goal through effort.

**Similar Words:**
- accomplish
- attain
- complete
- succeed

**Antonyms:**
- fail
- lose

**Simple Example:**
> She worked hard to achieve her dream.

**Common Collocations:**
- achieve success
- achieve a goal
- achieve results
- achieve excellence

**Business/Technical Example:**
> Our team achieved a 99.99% application uptime after improving system monitoring.

**Speaking Tip:**
Native speakers often say:
> "We achieved our target."

**Common Mistakes:**
> ❌ I achieved to finish the task.

> ✅ I achieved my goal.

> ✅ I managed to finish the task.

**Word Family:**
- Verb: achieve
- Noun: achievement
- Adjective: achievable
- Adverb: achievably (rare)
