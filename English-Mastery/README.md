Welcome to my **English Mastery Journey**.

This repository documents my commitment to improving my English vocabulary, communication, business English, and technical English through consistent daily learning.

My goal is not just to learn new words, but to confidently use them in daily conversations, professional meetings, technical discussions, presentations, and leadership communication.

---

# 🎯 Goals

- Improve spoken English fluency
- Build a strong vocabulary
- Enhance business communication
- Master technical English for Software Engineering
- Improve writing skills
- Speak confidently in meetings and presentations
- Communicate naturally with global teams

---

# 📅 Learning Plan

- 📖 Learn **5 new words every day**

# 📆 Weekly Routine

### Monday – Friday

- Learn 5 new words
- Practice pronunciation
- Create one sentence for each word

### Saturday

- Revise all words learned during the week
- Complete a short quiz

### Sunday

- Write one paragraph using the week's vocabulary
- Practice speaking for 2–3 minutes

---

# 📅 Monthly Routine

At the end of every month:

- Review all learned words
- Revise difficult vocabulary
- Practice conversation
- Complete a vocabulary assessment
